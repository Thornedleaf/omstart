Welcome to Omstart Out of the Void

Color blindness tests are important because they can help people understand how they see and distinguish different colors.
Some people may have color vision deficiency without knowing it, which can sometimes make everyday activities, schoolwork, or certain tasks more difficult.
Testing can therefore help people become more aware of how their color vision works.

It is also important to understand that this project is only a school project.
The tests are made for educational purposes and are not intended to provide a medical diagnosis.
There are no doctors, medical professionals, or other healthcare experts behind this project.
If someone has concerns about their vision or color perception, they should always contact a qualified healthcare professional for a proper examination.
The purpose of this project is simply to learn more about color blindness, how color vision tests work, and why they can be useful.
